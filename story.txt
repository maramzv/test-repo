The lighthouse keeper vanished on a night when the sea was too calm to explain it. No storm, no ship in distress, no note left behind—just an open door and a lamp still burning. Detective Mara Voss had seen strange cases before, but never one where the only witness was the tide. She arrived on the island before the ferry had even finished docking.

Turns out that on further inspection there really was another witness, the lone fishermen known only as the night catcher, who only goes fishing at night. He saw the lighthouse from his view on the beach and maybe he could tell the detective more about what happened. When she went to ask him, he was relunctant to share as most of the village had never given him the time of day and now all of a sudden he was the only one who would have any remarks to the wehereabouts of the keeper. This nighttime catcher would be the hero in the end.

Following the night catcher's directions, Mara followed a trail of wet footprints from the lighthouse to a small cave hidden beneath the cliffs. Inside, she found the missing keeper alive, guarding a weathered wooden box he had discovered beneath the lighthouse. He confessed that he had disappeared because someone had been watching him ever since he opened it. Suddenly, footsteps echoed from the entrance of the cave. Mara reached for her flashlight and realized the mystery was far from over.

The footsteps stopped just outside the cave. Mara switched off her flashlight and listened. The keeper whispered that the person approaching was the same stranger he had seen watching the lighthouse for weeks. When a shadow appeared at the entrance, Mara raised her flashlight and called out. The figure turned and ran, leaving behind a soaked jacket containing a map of the island and a photograph of the wooden box. Mara realized the keeper had not disappeared at all—he had been hiding to protect whatever was inside the box. And now, whoever wanted it knew exactly where to find him.
