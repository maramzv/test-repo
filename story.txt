The lighthouse keeper vanished on a night when the sea was too calm to explain it. No storm, no ship in distress, no note left behind—just an open door and a lamp still burning. Detective Mara Voss had seen strange cases before, but never one where the only witness was the tide. She arrived on the island before the ferry had even finished docking.

Mara followed a trail of wet footprints from the lighthouse to a small cave hidden beneath the cliffs. Inside, she found the missing keeper alive, guarding a weathered wooden box he had discovered beneath the lighthouse. He confessed that he had disappeared because someone had been watching him ever since he opened it. Suddenly, footsteps echoed from the entrance of the cave. Mara reached for her flashlight and realized the mystery was far from over.
